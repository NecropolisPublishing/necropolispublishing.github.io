Lectern™

A quiet room for words.

Lectern is our long-form writing desktop app: a distraction-free room for books, essays, and drafts. It keeps your manuscript in plain markdown, on your own machine.

  • Book Mode: your manuscript laid out as printed pages
  • Markdown-clean, with export to Word, PDF, and EPUB
  • A built-in Compendium for characters, places, and lore
  • Optional AI and Voice, using your own keys
  • Local-first. Your files stay yours.
